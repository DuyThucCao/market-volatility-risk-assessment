# Federal Reserve FOMC Press Releases

24 official FOMC monetary policy statements spanning the full 2022–2024 rate cycle, downloaded directly from the Federal Reserve.

| Period | Meetings | Policy Action |
|--------|----------|---------------|
| Jan–Dec 2022 | 8 meetings | Rate hikes from 0–0.25% to 4.25–4.50% (+425 bps) |
| Jan–Dec 2023 | 8 meetings | Further hikes to 5.25–5.50% (+100 bps), then hold |
| Jan–Dec 2024 | 8 meetings | Hold through July; cuts begin Sep 2024 (−100 bps) |

These documents are used to construct the FOMC event calendar in the analysis. Each release date is mapped to the nearest subsequent trading day and flagged as an event in the macro calendar. The primary source language (e.g., the shift from *"ongoing increases appropriate"* in 2022 to *"holding steady allows assessment"* in June 2023 to *"gained greater confidence"* in September 2024) is documented in the main README's FOMC Rate Decision Chronology table.
