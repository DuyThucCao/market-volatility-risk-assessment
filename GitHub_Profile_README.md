## Featured Projects

### 📉 Quantitative Risk Assessment of Market Volatility Under Macroeconomic Impacts
> Modeled volatility for a **$1,000,000 simulated multi-asset portfolio** (SPY, XLK, XLF, XLV, DJI, VIX) across 2022–2024.  
> Mapped FOMC rate decisions, CPI, and GDP releases to identify macroeconomic volatility drivers.  
> GARCH(1,1) with Student-t distribution captured fat-tail behavior; VaR backtesting (Kupiec Test, p > 0.05) confirmed statistical validity.

`Python` `ARIMA(1,1,0)` `GARCH(1,1)` `VaR` `yfinance` `FRED` `ADF/KPSS` `Kupiec Backtest`

---

### 📈 Forecasting GDP Growth with Shrinkage Methods and Diffusion Indices
> Built a **pseudo out-of-sample GDP forecasting system** comparing 6 models (Naive Mean, Random Walk, Ridge, Lasso, PCA Diffusion Index) across 1Q and 4Q horizons using 20 macroeconomic time series from FRED.  
> Implemented expanding-window cross-validation; found big-data methods outperform the random walk at both horizons.

`Python` `Ridge Regression` `Lasso` `PCA` `FRED API` `MSFE / MAE benchmarks`

---

### 🎓 Student Performance Analysis
> Cleaned a comprehensive student dataset, flagged at-risk learners by GPA tier, and built interactive visuals linking major, grades, and test scores.  
> Translated findings into targeted tutoring and resource-allocation recommendations for academic advisors.

`R` `ggplot2` `tidyverse` `Exploratory Data Analysis`

---

### 🏥 Healthcare Billing Prediction
> Analyzed a 1,000+ row healthcare dataset using SQL and R to evaluate admission types and billing patterns.  
> Applied hypothesis testing (permutation tests, z-tests) and confidence intervals to reach statistically valid conclusions on cost variation.

`R` `SQL` `ggplot2` `Hypothesis Testing` `Statistical Inference`
